# Current rear I/O mechanical study: A8

The owner now requires one **horizontal PCB with every connector directly soldered to it**, including the conventional speaker clips. This supersedes the vertical carrier and optical daughterboards.

Read [A8 dimensions, parts, sourcing and verification](horizontal-assembly-A8.md). Use the [rigid Fusion assembly](../boards/rear-io/exports/rear-io-A8-rigid.f3d) for enclosure integration. The component side faces down; the connector seating plane is at z67 mm, and solder tails point up.

A8 is a fit experiment. The KLS speaker block's current rating and purchasing route remain unresolved. Plate cutouts, the 0.2 mm PCB setback, support hardware, cable access, tolerances and thermal behavior are not qualified. No circuitry or manufacturing release exists.

The [A7 mechanical record](rear-io-mechanical-A7.md), exports and Git history remain available for comparison.
