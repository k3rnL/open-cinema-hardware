# Open Cinema hardware guidance

- This repository owns editable implementation. The sibling `../open-cinema-resources` owns durable memory. Read its reference index and relevant topics first; use its `.agents/skills/open-cinema-memory/SKILL.md` for memory updates.
- Current A7 uses two Cliff FE6930 eight-contact blocks: six working pairs plus two owner-approved spares, published7 A at 60 V AC. PT-603B is superseded; A1 industrial terminals, A2 downward tails and the3 A WP6-03 alternative are rejected.
- The vertical main board body is 228 × 32 × 1.6; four R4 alignment tabs, two per side, make overall width 238. Updated owner opening is 220 × 35 R5, z34…69, measured read-only; A2's upward-opening assumption and top-hole conflict are superseded. Read `docs/rear-io-mechanical.md` before integration.
- Main speaker pads and four component-fixing NPTH holes are provisional drawing-derived geometry (2.2x3.2 plated slots; component fixing pitch58). They are not owner frame mounting holes. The owner explicitly requested actual Ø3 alignment holes on both sides, at x±115,z44/59. HREF1/HREF2 are left; HREF3/HREF4 are right. All four must appear in STEP exports. Mounting hardware remains undecided.
- Preserve the vertical main-PCB choice. Optical receivers use small horizontal daughterboards. Main J7/J8 are mechanical assembly instances; actual receiver pads are in `boards/optical-input`. A6 added a shared bolted prototype cradle and two M2 holes per child; interconnects remain deferred. A7 translates the optics/cradle4 mm right. Read `docs/assembly-and-optical-support-A7.md`.
- Distinguish vendor/library CAD from project-reconstructed models. Read `docs/connector-selection-A7.md` for speaker assumptions and `docs/connector-selection-A1.md` for audio-input sources.
- No circuitry or manufacturing release exists. Keep mains separate; do not common speaker negatives or tie them to chassis by assumption.
- Enclosure placement uses A7 rigid STEP/F3D: one component/135 bodies. Raw KiCad STEP is hierarchical. Disable Ground to Parent for a joint; preserve four owner alignment holes.
- JLCPCB is preferred, not exclusive: owner allows Global Sourcing/pre-order and external suppliers for difficult parts. Audit `docs/sourcing-A7.md`; never label consignment or distributor stock as confirmed JLC stock.
- Do not regenerate over manual edits. A7 exports use `tools/export_review.py`; checks use `check_connectors_A7.py` plus both A7 Fusion verifiers. `revise_rear_io_A7.py --apply` is a one-time A6 migration. Older scripts/evidence are historical.
- Verify native files, pad geometry, model paths and exported solids. DRC without nets does not establish circuit completeness, enclosure fit or fabrication suitability.
- Keep project-relative paths. Exclude personal paths, caches, locks, account data and transient downloads.
