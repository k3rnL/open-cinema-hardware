# Open Cinema hardware guidance

- This repository owns editable implementation. The sibling `../open-cinema-resources` owns durable memory. Read its reference index and relevant topics first; use its `.agents/skills/open-cinema-memory/SKILL.md` for memory updates.
- Current A6 retains A3's mechanical evaluation of two Dragon City PT-603B straight-tail red/black six-contact blocks. Current rating, supply and production footprint remain unqualified. A1 Phoenix terminals and A2 KLS downward tails are rejected.
- The vertical main board body is 228 × 32 × 1.6; four R4 alignment tabs, two per side, make overall width238. Updated owner opening is 220 × 35 R5, z34…69, measured read-only; A2's upward-opening assumption and top-hole conflict are superseded. Read `docs/rear-io-mechanical.md` before integration.
- Main speaker pads and four component-fixing NPTH holes are provisional drawing-derived geometry. They are not owner frame mounting holes. The owner explicitly requested actual Ø3 alignment holes on both sides, at x±115,z44/59. HREF1/HREF2 are left; HREF3/HREF4 are right. All four must appear in STEP exports. Mounting hardware remains undecided.
- Preserve the vertical main-PCB choice. Optical receivers use small horizontal daughterboards. Main J7/J8 are mechanical assembly instances; actual receiver pads are in `boards/optical-input`. A6 adds a shared bolted prototype cradle and two M2 holes per child; interconnects remain deferred. Read `docs/assembly-and-optical-support-A6.md`.
- Distinguish vendor/library CAD from project-reconstructed models. Read `docs/connector-selection-A3.md` for speaker assumptions and `docs/connector-selection-A1.md` for audio-input sources.
- No circuitry or manufacturing release exists. Keep mains separate; do not common speaker negatives or tie them to chassis by assumption.
- Enclosure placement uses A6 rigid STEP/F3D: one component/123 bodies. Raw KiCad STEP is hierarchical. Disable Ground to Parent for a joint; preserve four owner alignment holes.
- JLCPCB is preferred, not exclusive: owner allows Global Sourcing/pre-order and external suppliers for difficult parts. Audit `docs/sourcing-A6.md`; never label consignment or distributor stock as confirmed JLC stock.
- Do not regenerate over manual edits. `mirror_alignment_holes_A5.py --apply` applies A5 to an A4 board; current A6 exports use `export_review.py` and checks use `check_connectors_A6.py`. Older generators/exporters/checkers are historical; do not overwrite historical evidence from the current board.
- Verify native files, pad geometry, model paths and exported solids. DRC without nets does not establish circuit completeness, enclosure fit or fabrication suitability.
- Keep project-relative paths. Exclude personal paths, caches, locks, account data and transient downloads.
